Shree Metal Packaging

Packaging that's built to last

Shree Metal Packaging is a tin printing and manufacturing facility. We run a 4-color UV offset metal decorating press and form the printed sheets into tins and caps in-house.

We manufacture sheet metal labels, tin containers, geometry box shells, medicine bottle caps, and paint can components for clients across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra.

Your nearest full-service tin printer

Most manufacturers sourcing tin packaging deal with a printer and a fabricator separately. We do both. That means fewer suppliers to manage, faster back-and-forth when specs change, and a single point of accountability for the finished product.

4-color UV offset metal printing Met Pet printing Aluminium PP cap making Metal can and box making Custom metal packaging
Facility photo

The colour is part of the metal. It does not peel.

Most tin products you see use a paper label applied over the tin. The label can peel, misalign, or absorb moisture. We print directly onto the metal sheet using 4-color CMYK UV offset before the sheet is formed. It holds through forming, handling, and the product's full shelf life.

Prepress, printing, forming, and quality check. All in-house.

From print file to finished tin, nothing leaves the facility. We handle prepress, printing, forming, and quality inspection internally.

Full CMYK. Not 1 or 2 colour.

Services

A diverse set of products

From geometry boxes and pencil tins to paint can components and medicine bottle caps, we manufacture a range of printed and formed metal products. Each is produced in-house.

Service photo

4-Color UV Metal Printing

For paint can labels, pharma components, FMCG packaging, and industrial containers. Share your print file and we confirm fit before quoting.

Service photo

Met Pet Printing

A metallized polyester film laminated onto carton board, giving packaging a high-gloss reflective finish. Combines the visual appeal of metallic packaging with the strength and workability of carton board. Used where premium shelf presence is the priority.

Service photo

Aluminium PP Cap Making

Aluminium polypropylene caps for medicine bottles, cosmetic containers, and industrial products. Available in standard sizes and custom profiles on request.

Service photo

Metal Can and Box Making

Tin containers and boxes in standard and custom sizes. Includes geometry boxes, pencil tins, ring-lid cans, and slip-lid tins for FMCG, food, stationery, and specialty applications.

Service photo

All Types of Metal Packaging

Custom metal packaging built to your dimensions, functional specs, and branding requirements. From prototype to full production run.

Printing for pharma, paint, FMCG, and industrial manufacturers

Our products go into regulated and demanding environments. Pharma clients need tamper-evident caps with clean sealing. Paint manufacturers need corrosion-resistant printed labels that survive the shelf. FMCG brands need multi-colour printed tins that compete at retail. We supply all of them.
